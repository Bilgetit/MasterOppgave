\begin{abstract}
  This thesis studies configurations of lines contained in algebraic surfaces in projective three-space $\mathbb{P}^3$. Several new configurations are defined and studied, and the existence of inclusions of these configurations in surfaces of varying degree is investigated, using both theoretical and computational results. 
 \end{abstract}
