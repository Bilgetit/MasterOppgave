\begin{conjecture}
    Let $C_m$ be a regular $m$-gon in $\mathbb{P}^3$, then we have 
    \[
        h^1(\mathbb{P}^3, \mathcal{I}_{C_m}(d)) = \mathrm{max}\left(0, \binom{d+3}{3} - md\right).
    \]
\end{conjecture}
This would mean that the bound we have found for the existence of surfaces containing the $m$-gon is sharp, and we would get the corollary:
\begin{corollary}
    For a regular $m$-gon $C_m$ in $\mathbb{P}^3$, we can not find any degree $d$ surface $S_d$ such that $C_m \subseteq S_d$ when $m \geq \binom{d+3}{3}\cdot \frac{1}{d}$.
\end{corollary}
