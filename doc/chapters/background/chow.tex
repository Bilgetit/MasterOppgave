\section{The Chow ring}

\cite{FUL84} Let $X$ be an algebraic scheme. A \emph{$k$-cycle} on $X$ is a finite formal sum 

\[
    \Sigma_i m_i V_i
\]

where the $V_i$ are $k$-dimensional subvarieties of $X$, and the $m_i$ are integers. The group of $k$-cycles on $X$, denoted $Z_k(X)$, is the free Abelian group generated by the $k$-cycles.




\begin{example}
    $(n-1)$-cycles are Weil divisors.
\end{example}
